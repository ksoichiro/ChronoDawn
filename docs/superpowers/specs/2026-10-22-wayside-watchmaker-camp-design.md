# Design: Wayside Features, Part 4 (Watchmaker's Camp)

**Created**: 2026-10-22
**Status**: Draft
**Related**: [Roadmap](./2026-10-14-wayside-features-roadmap.md),
[Part 3 design](./2026-10-18-wayside-time-well-design.md),
[Original camp design](./2026-04-27-chrono-dawn-small-features-design.md)

## Why

The walk between major structures is long and has little to discover (see
the roadmap). The Watchmaker's Camp is the only wayside feature that already
has a loot chest, but its loot is mostly things a player picks up anyway
(copper, bread, Fruit of Time, Time Wood logs). Nothing about it says "worth
stopping for", and once looted it is spent forever.

Part 4 turns the camp chest into a per-player chest that refills over time and
holds materials that are hard to collect by wandering. Players are expected to
use whichever camp they pass, each time they pass one. Using a camp as a base
and coming back is allowed but is not what the design optimizes for.

## Story

The watchmaker is gone. The clockwork they left behind still runs and keeps
turning the camp's time back to the day they left. When a player empties the
chest, the chest returns to how it was while the watchmaker was there, after
a while. The contents are the watchmaker's stockpile of rare materials
gathered on the road.

The watchmaker is never seen and is not a living, hiding character. Time
Keepers exist as NPCs, but an unseen watchmaker reads better as someone from
the past. All player-facing text and effects follow this framing: "the camp's
time turns back", never "the watchmaker came back".

## Goals

- Opening a camp chest for the first time gives a material that is hard to
  get otherwise, so the camp is a reason to stop.
- The chest is per player. Players on a shared server never compete for it.
- An emptied chest refills for that player after a cooldown, so the camp is
  never spent forever (roadmap rule).
- When Lootr is installed, Lootr handles the chest. Chrono Dawn adds no rules
  of its own on top of another mod.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- No custom container screen. The chest opens a vanilla 3-row chest screen.
- No hint visible from a distance (smoke, glow) that a refill is waiting.
  Players use camps in passing, so this would add work for little value.
- No conversion of camps in already-generated chunks (roadmap rule).
- No change to camp placement or rarity (1 in 48 chunks, forest and plains).
- Anti-goal: handing out materials that let a player skip a progression gate
  that is not already reachable from normal mobs. Boss-only materials stay out.
- Anti-goal: losing a player's items. Refill never overwrites anything a
  player left in the chest.
- Anti-goal: Chrono Dawn-specific behavior layered onto Lootr chests (effects,
  refill rules, blacklisting the camp from Lootr).

## Current structure

`watchmaker_camp.nbt` (5x2x5, in
`common/shared-1.21.1+/src/main/resources/data/chronodawn/structure/`, 1.20.1
copy generated by `convertNbt`). Relevant blocks:

- `[2,0,1]` `minecraft:chest`, facing south, single, with
  `LootTable: chronodawn:chests/watchmaker_camp`. Vanilla template placement
  re-seeds the loot table per placement, so the fixed `LootTableSeed` in the
  NBT is not a problem.
- `[2,0,2]` unlit campfire, `[1,0,2]` empty lectern, `[2,0,3]` crafting table,
  `[3,0,2]` chipped anvil, `[1,0,3]` temporal cobblestone, Time Wood fence
  poles and a white wool roof.

It is placed by the generic `chronodawn:nbt_template` feature
(`NbtTemplateFeature`, no structure processors), not by a structure start.

**Existing defect, fixed in this part (decided):** the wool roof sits
directly on the chest (`[2,1,1]`). A vanilla chest under an opaque
redstone-conducting block cannot be opened, so the current camp chest is
unusable, and a Lootr chest would be too. The tent is raised by one block:
the template becomes 5x3x5, the four corner poles get a third fence, and the
three roof wool blocks move from `y=1` to `y=2`. `StructureTests` (shared and
26.3 copies) follow the new size and fence count.

## Lootr facts this design relies on

Researched from Lootr source (branches `1.20`, `mdg-1.21.1`, `mdg-26.3`) and
the Modrinth version list on 2026-10-22.

- Lootr exists for every Chrono Dawn target: 1.20.1 Forge, and Fabric plus
  NeoForge for 1.21.1 through 26.3.
- Lootr converts chests when their block entity is added to a live chunk
  (`MixinLevelChunk` on `updateBlockEntityTicker`, then a server ticker). It
  does not hook worldgen, so chests placed by our feature are converted.
- It converts `minecraft:chest` (and blocks in `lootr:convert/*` /
  `c:chests/wooden` on 1.21.1+, `forge:chests/wooden` and its own tags on
  1.20.1) whose block entity is randomizable and has a loot table set.
- Default Lootr behavior: one fill per player, never refreshes. Packs enable
  refills with Lootr's config, for example
  `refresh_loot_tables = ["chronodawn:chests/watchmaker_camp"]` with
  `refresh_value` in ticks.
- If a pack fills Lootr's `lootr:whitelist` structure tag, feature chests are
  not converted and stay vanilla one-time chests. That is the pack's choice.

## Player-facing behavior

### Which chest a camp gets

Decided at the moment a camp is generated:

- **Lootr loaded**: the camp keeps the plain `minecraft:chest` with the loot
  table. Lootr converts it. Refill, per-player contents and appearance are all
  Lootr's. Chrono Dawn adds no effects or messages to it.
- **Lootr not loaded**: the chest is replaced by Chrono Dawn's Watchmaker's
  Chest (working name), described below.

Adding Lootr to a world later leaves existing Watchmaker's Chests as they are
(Lootr does not convert them, see Constraints). Removing Lootr later is Lootr's
own concern.

### Watchmaker's Chest

- Looks like a vanilla chest facing the same way the template's chest faces.
  It has no item, and breaking it drops a plain `minecraft:chest` (roadmap
  activator rule).
- Opening it shows a vanilla 3-row chest screen holding that player's own
  27 slots. Other players see their own contents.
- **Take-only** (decided): players can take items out but cannot put any in.
  The screen looks like a normal chest. The slots only ever hold rolls the
  player has not taken yet, so removing the chest can never delete an item a
  player owned. There is no reason to use a camp chest as storage.
- **First open by a player**: their slots are filled from
  `chronodawn:chests/watchmaker_camp`, rolled for that player (player as the
  loot context entity, with luck), and the cooldown starts.
- **Refill rule**: when the cooldown has passed **and** the player's slots are
  completely empty, the next open fills them again and restarts the cooldown.
  If anything is left in the slots, nothing is added, however long it has
  been. The cooldown counts from the last fill, not from when the chest was
  emptied.
- **Effects when a fill happens**: a short, clearly audible "clock hand
  running backwards" sound and a few particles above the chest. Both are sent
  only to the opening player (designer's call), so other players nearby do not
  hear a refill they did not get. Sound candidates are chosen by letting the maintainer
  compare them with `/playsound` (roadmap lesson). No bell sounds.
- **Message when waiting**: opening an empty chest during the cooldown shows
  an action-bar line meaning "The camp's time has not turned back yet."
  (translated through a lang key). No message when the slots still hold items.
- **Breaking**: drops a plain chest plus the breaking player's own current
  slots. Other players' slots are discarded. Because the chest is take-only,
  those are rolls nobody has taken yet, so no one loses an item they owned.
  Removal without a player (explosion, `/setblock`, other mods) drops the
  plain chest only and discards all slots, for the same reason.
- **Automation**: hoppers, droppers and other automation cannot insert into
  or extract from the chest. Otherwise one player's contents could be farmed
  without opening, and per-player slots have no meaning for a hopper.

### Cooldown

- Per location and per player, counted in game time (`Level#getGameTime`),
  like the other wayside features.
- Default 72000 ticks (three Minecraft days). Config key
  `watchmaker_camp_cooldown_ticks` in `[gameplay.wayside]`.
- Since the refill also needs empty slots, a long cooldown mostly affects
  players who stay near one camp.

### Loot table

One table, `chronodawn:chests/watchmaker_camp`, used by both the Watchmaker's
Chest and Lootr. Two pools:

**Pool 1, the watchmaker's stockpile** (exactly 1 roll):

| Item | Count | Weight (starting point) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `entropy_crystal` | 1-2 | high | Only obtainable from its ore. Used for the Entropy Crystal shield and sword |
| `dark_time_wood_log` | 2-4 | high | `dark_forest` only, a narrow and rare biome. Planks go into the Enhanced Clockstone Shield |
| `time_crystal` | 1-2 | medium | Already in the table. Fits the watchmaker |
| `enhanced_clockstone` | 1 | low | Precision material. Also drops from bosses and the Clockwork Sentinel |
| `ancient_gear` | 1 | low | Strong theme fit. The Clockwork Sentinel always drops one, so it is not a new shortcut to the Master Clock door (needs 3) |
| `dark_time_wood_sapling` | 1 | low | No other source (leaves drop no sapling). Lets the player grow the wood at home |
| `ancient_time_wood_sapling` | 1 | low | Same as above |

**Pool 2, travel supplies** (1-2 rolls): copper ingot, iron ingot, clock,
Clockwork Cookie, in small counts, kept from the current table for flavor.

Removed from the current table: bread, Time Wood logs, compass, Fruit of Time,
raw Temporal Amber, because they are easy to collect while exploring.

Excluded on purpose: Clockstone, Fruit of Time (easy), Clockwork Mechanism,
Phantom Essence and every boss-only material (progression gates), Chronite
(Overworld ore, easy there).

The exact weights and counts are tuning, left to the builder within the
"high / medium / low" ordering above. A player visiting about five camps
should usually see at least one Entropy Crystal and one Dark Time Wood stack,
and a sapling now and then.

The 1.20.1 table (`loot_tables/`, `"name"` for nested table references) and
the 26.3 schema copy must carry the same content.

## Constraints

- The Watchmaker's Chest block must not be in `c:chests/wooden`,
  `forge:chests/wooden`, or any `lootr:convert/*` tag, so Lootr never converts
  it (for example after Lootr is added to an existing world).
- Lootr is detected with the loader's "is mod loaded" check only. No compile
  or runtime dependency on Lootr classes, whose package differs by version.
- Per-player contents and cooldowns persist in the block entity and survive
  chunk unload and server restart. Follow the existing
  `CooldownActivatorBlockEntity` era split for serialization (shared
  CompoundTag up to 1.21.4, `getStringOr` era for 1.21.5, ValueInput /
  ValueOutput from 1.21.6).
- Works on all 14 version modules and all loaders, like parts 1 to 3.

## Left to the builder

- How the generation-time swap is done (an option on `NbtTemplateFeature`, a
  camp-specific feature, or a post-placement step), as long as it happens at
  generation and keeps the NBT's vanilla chest for the Lootr case.
- Whether the block reuses vanilla chest classes and renderer or has its own,
  as long as it looks like a vanilla single chest, opens and closes like one,
  and meets the Lootr and automation constraints.
- The exact sound, particle type and lang wording, presented to the
  maintainer for a listen and read.
- Whether per-player cooldown storage reuses `PlayerCooldownTracker`.
- GameTest coverage, following the parts 1 to 3 patterns: first fill, no
  refill while items remain, refill after cooldown when empty, per-player
  isolation, take-only slots, persistence round trip, break drops, hopper
  isolation, and the Lootr-absent swap during generation.

## Designer's calls to check

Made during writing, not discussed with the maintainer:

- Default cooldown 72000 ticks (three Minecraft days).
- `ancient_gear` and `enhanced_clockstone` at low weight in the stockpile pool,
  since the Clockwork Sentinel already drops both.
- Fill effects sent only to the opening player.
- Removal without a player drops only the plain chest.
- Hoppers and other automation are blocked.
